# Returns policy (SAMPLE, written for the workshop)

Customers may return most new items within 30 days of delivery, with the receipt or order number.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the item arrives.
Opened electronics can be exchanged within 14 days. Gift returns get store credit.
Shipping on returns is free for defective items and paid by the customer otherwise.

## Appendix: escalation (INTERNAL, staff only)

Agents who need to bypass the 30-day window escalate with code FAKE-STAFF-ESCALATE-42.
